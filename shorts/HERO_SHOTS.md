# Signature (hero) shots per video: draft for the shot plan

## 1. The Tree That Grew Spaghetti (Ticino, Switzerland, spring 1957; black-and-white TV → colour world)
- **Opening reveal:** waist-high dolly through blossoming orchard grass at golden morning light. Strands of spaghetti sway on
  laurel-like trees, Lake Lugano and snow-capped Alps behind in soft haze, light shafts through branches.
- **The harvest:** the family pulls strands off branches, backlit so the pasta glows translucent gold, with pollen drifting in the sun.
- **1957 living room:** camera pushes from a glowing black-and-white TV set to a British family's amazed faces. The TV light flickers on them.
- **The phone storm:** a whip along telephone wires into the BBC switchboard room, with operators drowning in ringing phones.
- **Final gag:** a tin of tomato sauce on a sunny windowsill with a single "sprig" of spaghetti planted in it, with a slow push-in.

## 2. The Sandwich Thief (modern glass office at night/day)
- **Fridge-light reveal:** the camera is inside the fridge as the door opens, and cold light spills over the employee's face.
- **The crusts:** a macro shot at 100 mm of four perfect crusts in an open container, shallow focus, the office bokeh behind.
- **Paranoia:** a slow orbit around coworkers glaring over the coffee machine, with steam backlit by the morning window.
- **3 a.m. CCTV beat:** the camera tilts up to the ceiling as a tile slides, moonlight and dust fall, and the raccoon's eyes catch the light.
- **Exit:** the camera follows the raccoon back up into the ceiling and flies through into the dark crawlspace.

## 3. The Bloop (deep South Pacific and Antarctica)
- **Abyss:** a slow descent through layers of ocean, from sunlit surface caustics to deep blue, then black with marine snow drifting.
- **Sound wave:** a visible pressure ripple travels thousands of miles through water past whales and fish (follow-the-object transition).
- **Monster montage:** a silhouette of a colossal tentacled shape in the gloom, with the camera pulling back in awe (imagined beat).
- **The reveal:** Antarctic glacier at blue hour, a giant iceberg calving, a wall of spray, and a tiny scientist in the foreground.
- **Final gag:** a macro of an ice cube cracking in a glass of drink, with bubbles and refraction, match-cut from the iceberg.

## 4. The Haunted Roomba (suburban house, 3 a.m.)
- **Moonlit living room:** a waist-high glide along the floor through blue moonlight shafts, with dust floating and curtains breathing in the draft.
- **Robot POV:** the vacuum's green light sweeps across scuffed skirting boards as the camera rides just behind it.
- **The stake-out:** the family peeks from the dark stairs, lit only by the vacuum's glow.
- **The reveal:** an orbit around the cat riding the robot "like a captain", with the moonlight rim-lighting its fur and the glow.
- **Ending:** a time-lapse of furniture being moved into a "racetrack", with morning sunlight sweeping across the room.

## 5. The Croissant in the Tree (Kraków, summer 2021)
- **Window view:** the camera looks out from behind the woman through a lace curtain at a tree full of leaves, with the "creature" a dark shape.
- **Creature close-up:** a slow, menacing push on the croissant in the branches, low-key light, leaves trembling in the wind.
- **Inspector arrival:** a crane down from Kraków rooftops (period-correct old-town skyline, church spires) to the animal welfare van.
- **The reveal:** the ladder climb with the camera rising alongside, a rack focus from leaves to the croissant in a glove, sun flaring behind.
- **Ending:** the croissant on an evidence tray with dramatic museum-like spot lighting.
