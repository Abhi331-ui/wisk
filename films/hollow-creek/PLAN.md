# HOLLOW CREEK TALES

**Logline:** In a sleepy 90s town, 12-year-old paper boy Milo Park loses his shadow one night. It runs off with a grin, and Milo finds out that every shadow in Hollow Creek is planning to leave its owner.

**Genre:** spooky adventure-comedy (suspense, light horror, action, heart). **Audience:** 10+ and nostalgic adults.
**Theme:** the parts of ourselves we ignore don't disappear; they go looking for attention.

## Cast
- **Milo Park (12):** paper boy and professional worrier. Round head, red cap, yellow raincoat, newspaper bag, blue bike.
- **The Shadow:** Milo's silhouette in violet-black, with white crescent eyes and a grin. Mischievous, not evil (at first).
- **Juniper "June" Ruiz (12):** the kid who reads every horror comic and believes Milo straight away. *(Part 1, p. 6)*
- **Mr. Grimsby:** the old projectionist at the shut-down Starlight Cinema, where the shadows gather. *(Part 2)*

## Five parts
1. **The Night My Shadow Ran Away:** the shadow escapes. A chase through town, June joins in. Cliffhanger: hundreds of shadows file into the Starlight Cinema.
2. **The Midnight Matinee:** Milo and June sneak into the cinema and meet Grimsby. The shadows are rehearsing to replace their owners.
3. **Lights Out:** the town's shadows switch places, and people start acting strange. Milo's shadow begins to doubt the plan.
4. **The Longest Night:** the shadows plan to put out every light in Hollow Creek forever. A siege at the water tower.
5. **Dawn:** Milo makes peace with his shadow, and sunrise brings the town back. Tag: June's shadow winks.

## Format and runtime
Each part has 30 pages, each page has 6 panels, and **every panel gets a 90-second 3D/2D scene**.
Each panel takes about 10 s on the page, plus 90 s in its scene, plus about 4 s of camera moves, so roughly 104 s.
That makes about 10.4 min per page, **about 5.2 h per part, and about 26 h for the whole story.**
